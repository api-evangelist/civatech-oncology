## Products

- [CivaSheet](https://civatechoncology.com/civasheet/): FDA-cleared, flexible, bioabsorbable, unidirectional planar brachytherapy source, custom-sizable in the operating room.
- [CivaString](https://civatechoncology.com/civastring/): Polymer-encapsulated brachytherapy seed used for prostate and other localized cancers.
- [CivaDerm](https://civatechoncology.com/civaderm/): Dermatological radiation product.
